Watch: STAYC Keeps It Real With Unfiltered Confidence In Summery “I WANT IT” MV

Updated July 21 KST:

STAYC is back with a fresh new summer track!

On July 21 at 6 p.m. KST, STAYC dropped the music video for their new title track “I WANT IT,” ahead of the album’s official release on various online music platforms on July 23.

“I WANT IT” is an addictive summer anthem that showcases STAYC’s signature energy. With the empowering message that “even my imperfections are my weapon,” the song exudes a cool confidence and a bold attitude about living true to oneself.

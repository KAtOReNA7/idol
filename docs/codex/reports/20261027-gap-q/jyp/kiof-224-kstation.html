Group KISS OF LIFE has released its first personal concept photo.

KISS OF LIFE released the first personal concept photos for its fourth mini-album “224” on its official SNS on May 26th at midnight KST. Starting with JULIE, the four members, NATTY, BELLE, and HANEUL showed their charisma and elegance by sporting intense smoky makeup and casual white outfits, attracting the attention of those who saw a refined charisma contrasting with their naturally cool side.

The members’ fixed, impassive gaze is particularly impressive. While the futuristic, trendy eyebrows under colorful makeup are eye-catching, the typographic phrases inscribed on each member’s outfits create a strange and lingering tension, further fueling curiosity for this new feature.

KISS OF LIFE, which has primarily showcased trendy styles since its debut, anticipated its transformation by seamlessly blending refined and urban styles on the same day.

KISS OF LIFE‘s new album, “224,” will be released on several online music platforms on June 9 at 6 p.m. KST.

Updated May 12 KST: 

MEOVV is here with new music!

On May 12 at 6 p.m. KST, MEOVV unveiled their first EP “MY EYES OPEN VVIDE” along with the music video for the title track.

“DROP TOP” features a cinematic music video that follows the members as they discover themselves, ultimately coming together as one. Starting with a calm, reflective mood, the pop song features rock elements and channels freedom and acceleration. MEOVV’s Gawon participating in writing the lyrics for the song.

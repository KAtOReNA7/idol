Original Article:

Mark your calendars: aespa is returning with new music next month!

On July 31 at midnight KST, aespa officially announced the date of their return with a brand-new summer release. The group will be dropping the single “Better Things” on August 18 at midnight in every local time zone.

The aespa members previously hinted to their fans that they would be releasing a new English-language single this summer, though SM Entertainment had not yet made an official announcement at the time.

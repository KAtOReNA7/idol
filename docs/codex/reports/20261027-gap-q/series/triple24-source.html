tripleS has finally made their return as a full group!

On May 8 at 6 p.m. KST, tripleS released their new album “ASSEMBLE24,” which is their first album as a full group, along with the music video for their title track “Girls Never Die.”

“Girls Never Die” contains lyrics about girls getting back up when they fall down. This is the story of tripleS which can also be expanded to the story of all girls of today.
